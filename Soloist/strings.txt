	EN	Delay Spotify to player

PLUGIN_SOLOIST_KEEP_DELAY
	EN	Keep the delay low

PLUGIN_SOLOIST_KEEP_DELAY_DESC
	EN	The Spotify source is live, so any backlog (after an LMS hiccup or a pause) stays as extra delay and makes Next and Pause react late. With this on, the plugin restarts the stream to drop the backlog: on a skip when the delay is over 2 s, when resuming after a pause, and at a track change when the delay is over 10 s.

PLUGIN_SOLOIST_MEASURE
	EN	Measure the Spotify stream

PLUGIN_SOLOIST_MEASURE_DESC
	EN	Checks the captured audio and shows what Spotify delivers: "Spotify LOSSLESS 16-bit" or "LOSSLESS 24-bit" (original values), "Spotify 16-bit (gain -3.9 dB)" or "24-bit (gain …)" (lossless source, but its level was changed, e.g. by Soloist's loudness normalisation), or "Spotify NOT BIT-PERFECT" (no integer grid: lossy or dithered). Shows plain "Spotify" until it is sure. Takes a few seconds per track; one line per track goes to soloist.log ("--- measure"). Applies from the next time the source starts.

PLUGIN_SOLOIST_AUTO_UPDATE
	EN	Update Soloist automatically

PLUGIN_SOLOIST_AUTO_UPDATE_DESC
	EN	Soloist builds stop working 90 days after their build date. When this is on, the plugin checks Spotify's download address once a day, downloads a newer build in the background, tests it, and switches to it when nothing has played through Soloist for 10 minutes (right away if the old build has expired). The previous build is kept as "soloist.prev" and restored automatically if the new one doesn't start. The download comes straight from Spotify to this machine.
